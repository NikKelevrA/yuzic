Music that plays to the end, and servers that hear about it.

- Tracks no longer stop part-way through and skip to the next song
- Seeking near the end of a track no longer ends it early
- Plex records what you play, and starring a track works — neither did before
- Jellyfin is told when you pause and when you stop, so it stops showing you as playing
- Landscape is now for tablets; phones stay upright
