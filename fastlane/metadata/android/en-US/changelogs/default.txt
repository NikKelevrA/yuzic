Buttons that work both ways, and settings that only offer what your server can do.

- A headphone or car button unpauses again, instead of only pausing
- With crossfade on, album segues stay hard-cut rather than fading through themselves
- Crossfaded tracks are scrobbled on what you heard, so they stop falling short
- Loudness and streaming quality appear only where your server supports them
- Jellyfin and Emby: an artist's top songs
- Playlists are found when search asks your server
