Large libraries are faster and far lighter.

- No more crashes syncing or reopening a library of 90,000 songs
- Opens from its saved copy instead of downloading your library again
- Sorting a big library is about six times faster
- Uses much less memory and storage
- A song that won't play no longer retries forever. After three in a row, playback stops and keeps your queue
- A song that failed behind hotel Wi-Fi or a broken proxy plays again once the connection is fixed
